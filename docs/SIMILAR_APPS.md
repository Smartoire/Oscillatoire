# Similar and Muse-compatible apps

A reference list for Brainwave: apps that work with a Muse headband, and apps that
do similar things (meditation, calm, focus, neurofeedback). Built from the sources
listed at the bottom. Descriptions come from those summaries, not from using each app,
so check them before relying on them. Sources that sell their own app (Muse, Flocus)
are promotional.

Brainwave's own position: a Muse visualiser that stays on the device, speaks in plain
language, and keeps the technical view behind an Advanced switch. It makes no medical claims.
It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.

## Muse-compatible apps

| App | Website | What it does (as reported) | Relevance to Brainwave | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Muse** (official, Brain Health & Sleep) | [choosemuse.com/pages/app](https://choosemuse.com/pages/app) | Real-time audio feedback from brain, body, heart and breath signals; guided meditations; sleep tracking and Smart Wakeup; weekly goals, challenges and post-session summaries. Premium adds content and cognitive tracking. | The reference app. Our time-in-state summary and calm session overlap with it. | Sleep staging is outside Brainwave's scope. Export and sharing are not mentioned. |
| **Myndlift** | [myndlift.com](https://www.myndlift.com/download-the-myndlift-app) | Neurofeedback with 1:1 coaching, EEG scans, and sessions that can run while streaming or playing games. Clinician tools with remote monitoring. Works with several Muse models. | Closest to clinical neurofeedback. | Calls itself "clinical-grade". We avoid medical claims. |
| **Flow Tracking** (Flocus) | [flocus.org](https://flocus.org/blog/muse-compatible-apps/) | Live 0–100 flow score during focus work, after a short calibration. Classifies states as Flow, Overload and Low Engagement. Tracks fatigue within a block. | Its state classes and fatigue tracking are worth borrowing. | Website is the Flocus blog that reviews it; I could not find the Flow Tracking product page. Says raw EEG is not stored or transmitted. Promotional source. |
| **Mind Monitor** | [Mind-Monitor.com](https://Mind-Monitor.com) | Real-time EEG display and recording for Muse headbands, including Athena. Records to iCloud Drive and streams over OSC. | Raw-data alternative for power users. | Developer: James Clutterbuck. The App Store listing says it is not an official Muse app. Listed as a Muse partner. |
| **Semse Aware** | [choosemuse.com/semse](https://choosemuse.com/semse) | Muse-compatible app with in-app premium subscriptions. A Mac version exists (macOS 11 or later, Apple M1 or newer). | Another calm and awareness app to compare with. | Website is Muse's product page for it. Subscription prices vary by country. |
| **OptiBrain** (Opti Brain) | [myoptibrain.com](https://www.myoptibrain.com) | Maps brain activity with Muse headbands, including Athena. Free for up to 10 data collections, then a subscription. Sister app to Opti Train, Opti Calm and Opti Confidence. | Brain-map style similar to ours. | Address from the Google Play listing. Reviews say the subscription is expensive. |
| **OptiTrain** (Opti Train) | [myoptibrain.com](https://www.myoptibrain.com) | Streams brain data, estimates how close you are to a target brain pattern, and gives audio or video feedback. Sessions can be viewed as brain maps in Opti Brain. Also works with Smith Lowdown Focus Glasses. | Target-pattern training with audio feedback. | Needs an Opti Brain subscription. One Google Play entry lists the developer as "Opti Zone", so the publisher may differ. |
| **Muse LSL** (open source) | [github.com/alexandrebarachant/muse-lsl](https://github.com/alexandrebarachant/muse-lsl) | Streams live EEG (plus PPG, accelerometer and gyro on Muse 2) into custom workflows over Lab Streaming Layer. | **BlueMuse** (open source, Windows) | [github.com/kowalej/BlueMuse](https://github.com/kowalej/BlueMuse) | Finds and connects Muse devices and streams them over LSL. | **MuseStudio** (open source library) | [Paper (Applied Sciences, 2021)](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/11/16/7644) | Python library for managing and visualising Muse data, BIDS compatible, with import and export. | Analysis side. | I could not find its repository; the paper is the best source. Not a consumer app. |
| **NeuroHarmony** (experimental) | Not found | Adaptive music driven by alpha, beta and theta, described in an earlier summary. | Same idea as our calm drone, but music changes with brain state. | I could not find a page for this name. | **Muse Health** (research platform) | [choosemuse.com](https://choosemuse.com) | Multi-night home sleep EEG for research, with raw and processed data export. Supports Muse 2, Muse S and Athena. Partnership with Cumulus Neuroscience for trials. | Export model for research users. | Company-reported accuracy claims. We avoid sleep staging. Website is Muse's main site. |

## Partner app details (from the Muse partner page and App Store listings)

These come from the App Store descriptions and version notes, read as text. Screenshots couldn't be read.

- **Mind Monitor** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mind-monitor/id988527143)): live spectrogram and
  band graphs, raw µV values, accelerometer and gyro graphs, and channel views (left/right, front/back, per sensor).
  Exports CSV or Muse Protocol Buffer (for Muse Player); saves to iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or streams over OSC.
  Records have a view on Mind-Monitor.com. Says it is not an official Muse app.
- **Myndlift** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/myndlift/id1448710522)): neurofeedback with games, videos or
  other streaming apps; scores, benchmarks and a brain assessment; CBT-based resources; a paid tier with remote supervision.
- **OptiBrain** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/opti-brain/id1029982015)): collect and name brain-map
  collections, 2D/3D and video maps, compare maps, share maps, Athena support. Ten free collections, then a subscription.
- **OptiTrain** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/opti-train/id1037469787)): sport-specific training (golf, archery,
  bowling and others) with audio or video feedback, a calibration screen, and a progress page. Needs OptiBrain.
- **Semse Aware** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/semse-aware/id1631181726)): scene-based sessions with
  audio-visual feedback, an evolving soundtrack, post-session graphs, and sessions written to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes.

Takeaways for Brainwave: CSV/JSON export with clear columns (Mind Monitor), a compare view (OptiBrain), a progress page
(OptiTrain), and Apple Health Mindful Minutes (Semse Aware) are all reasonable next steps. Each needs a check against the
SDK terms before it goes in.
